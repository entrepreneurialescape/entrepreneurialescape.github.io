Privacy Policy

Planets Merge · GZ Media · Last updated 21 October 2026

The short version. Planets Merge has no accounts and no sign-up. Your progress, your chosen name and your best scores are stored on your own device, not on our servers. We operate no servers and we receive no personal information about you. Two Google services inside the game do collect data, and those are described below.

What stays on your device

The game saves the following locally, in your device's app storage:

  • Your game progress, best scores and unlocked power-ups
  • The display name and country flag you choose for the local leaderboard
  • Your settings, such as sound, music and background theme
  • An in-progress game, so you can close the app and resume later

None of this is transmitted to us. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Google AdMob (advertising)

The game shows ads supplied by Google AdMob, including an ad after a game ends and optional ads you can choose to watch in exchange for a power-up charge. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier and related technical information in order to serve and measure ads.

We never see this data. It is handled by Google under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Google Play Games Services (leaderboards)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your score and your Play Games player identity (your chosen Play Games display name and avatar) are sent to Google so you can appear on the worldwide leaderboards. Those leaderboards are hosted by Google, not by us.

Signing in is optional. If you decline, the game works normally and your scores simply stay on your device. You can manage or delete your Play Games data at myaccount.google.com.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location
  • We do not create accounts or require a sign-up
  • We do not run analytics of our own
  • We do not sell or share data, because we do not collect any

Children

Planets Merge is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the ads or Play Games features, please contact us and we will help you raise it with Google.

Your choices

  • Delete everything the game has stored by uninstalling it
  • Decline Play Games sign-in to keep scores on your device only
  • Reset your advertising ID in your device settings to limit ad personalisation

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
